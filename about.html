About Danyx Studio

Gospel production with heart, skill and an X-Factor.

A producer’s story from Maua

Danyx Studio began with a simple conviction: gospel artists in Maua deserve a studio that sounds as good as their message. As a producer and musician, Danyx built this room so local voices could be heard clearly, on radio, in church and online.


Today the studio records, mixes, masters and composes, and teaches the next generation of pianists and singers.
